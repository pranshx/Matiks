The problem

The evidence
What the best figured out.
Chess.com
The number is your face
Rating lives on your profile picture. Every rank has a name. The community decided what 1200 means. The number has social weight because they built meaning around it for years.
Clash Royale
Status is visible inside the app
Cosmetics, emotes, arena themes · none make you better. All make you look like someone worth noticing. Where there is social, people want status. They monetize identity, not gameplay.
Duolingo
Loss aversion converts
Question 9 of 10. One heart left. People don't pay to win · they pay to not lose what they've already built. The purchase happens at peak emotional investment.
Matiks has a live opponent on the other side of the loss. That's more painful than failing a Duolingo lesson. The emotional leverage is stronger · it's just not being used yet.
The thesis
Three unlocks.
One insight.
01
Meaning layer · make the number matter on day one
Show percentile. Name the ranks. Show proximity to next tier on the defeat screen. "1020 · Top 34% of Math Sprint players. 18 points to Silver." Now the -2 means something. Now the rematch button has a real reason.
02
Cosmetic identity · make status visible inside the app
A 365-day streak holder looks identical to a day-one user inside the app. Profile frames, rating badges, custom duel animations. The T-shirt lives in the physical world. Nothing lives in the digital one yet.
03
Rating shield · monetize the loss aversion moment
On a 3-game win streak. Rating climbing. Spend Pie before a high-stakes duel to protect your rating if you lose. You don't pay to win · you pay to not lose what you earned. Purchase at peak emotional investment.
